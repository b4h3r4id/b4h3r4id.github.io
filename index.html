About Bd ProTech

Bd ProTech is an independent Android app publisher. We build small, fast apps that do one job well — and keep improving them with regular updates.

Every app has its own public privacy policy and a Data safety section on its Google Play page, so you always know what an app does with your data.

  • Lightweight by design

    Small downloads and quick start-up, so the apps stay fast on everyday phones.

  • Simple and focused

    One clear purpose per app, clean Material design and no complicated menus.

  • Clear about privacy

    A privacy policy for every app, plus the Data safety details on Google Play.

  • Actively maintained

    Regular updates with fixes, new themes and features based on user feedback.

Frequently asked questions

Are Bd ProTech apps free?

Yes — every app is free to download from Google Play. Scoreboard, Chess Clock and Network Manager show ads, and Network Manager offers an optional Premium upgrade.

Which Android apps does Bd ProTech make?

Wind Browser: Fast & Light (a lightweight web browser), Scoreboard - Count Score (a score counter for sports and board games), Chess Clock - Timer (a chess clock for Blitz, Bullet and Rapid) and Network Manager: Data usage (a data usage and internet speed monitor). Wavy: Music Player is coming soon.

Where can I read the privacy policies?

Every app has its own page: Wind Browser, Scoreboard, Chess Clock, Network Manager and Wavy.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Email bd.protech.corp@gmail.com or leave a review on the app's Google Play page. We're happy to help.

What is Wavy: Music Player?

Wavy is our upcoming offline music player for Android. It is currently in testing and will be announced on this page when it launches on Google Play.

Questions, bugs or ideas?

We read feedback to decide what to improve next. Write to us by email, or rate and review the apps on Google Play.
