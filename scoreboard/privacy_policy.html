Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the ScoreBoard app (package name com.bd.protech.scoreboard, hereby referred to as “Application”) created by BD Protech (“we”, “us”, the “Developer”). The Application is an ad-supported service with an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes ads. This service is intended for use “AS IS”.

1. Information We Collect

We (the Developer) do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. The Application has no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not operate servers for the Application, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.

However, the third-party services used by the Application (described in sections 3 and 4) may process certain device information. That data is collected by those companies, not by the Developer.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The Application saves the following only on your device: team names, scores and rounds, game rules (such as win limit, points per tap, round limit and round timer), board layout, sound, vibration and confetti switches, theme and color choices, and whether you have purchased “Remove Ads”. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you clear the Application’s data or uninstall it.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, the system may include this data in your Google backup. That backup is managed by Google and Android, not by the Developer.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may use your device’s Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device and operating system information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and show personalized or non-personalized advertisements. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the Developer.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you purchase “Remove Ads”, the Application stops requesting and showing ads.

For more information, please check the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Google Advertising)
  • How Google uses data when you use partner apps

4. In-App Purchase and Other Services

  • Google Play Billing (“Remove Ads”): Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The Application only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase (for example after reinstalling) and keeps a local “ads removed” status on your device. Refunds and purchase issues are handled under Google Play’s policies.
  • GitHub (update check): When the Application starts, it downloads a small text file containing the latest version number from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) to tell you if an update is available. The Application sends no personal information in this request, but, as with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • This web page: The “Live Statistics” chart at the bottom of this page is provided by chrome-stats.com and is loaded only when you visit this page, not inside the Application.

5. App Permissions

The Application requires minimal permissions to function:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to load ads (AdMob), verify your purchase with Google Play, check for updates, and open external links (e.g., Privacy Policy, Rate App).
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Allows AdMob to use the Advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement.
  • Google Play Billing (BILLING): Used to process and restore the “Remove Ads” purchase.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for haptic feedback when scores change and when the timer ends.

No sensitive permissions (Camera, Microphone, Storage, Contacts, Phone, Location) are requested.

6. Data Sharing, Retention and Deletion

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it. The only parties that receive device data through the Application are the third-party services listed above. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. To remove all data stored on your device, clear the Application’s data or uninstall it.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings.
  • Remove ads permanently with the “Remove Ads” purchase.
  • Clear the Application’s data or uninstall it at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, such requests generally concern Google or GitHub, and you can use their privacy controls. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help you with any request.

8. Children’s Privacy

This Application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
