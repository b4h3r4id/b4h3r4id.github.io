Wind Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · App: Wind Player (com.bd.protech.windplayer)

Wind Player is an offline-first music player for Android. It plays the audio files that are already on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services. This page explains the small number of cases where the app uses the network, and what stays on your device.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your music library — the app reads your audio files through Android's media storage (permission to read audio). File names, tags and cover art are used only to show and play your music; they are never uploaded.
  • Listening history and statistics, favorites, playlists, queue and settings — stored locally in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Custom cover art, imported lyrics and lyrics you chose from an online search — stored locally in the app's private storage (excluded from cloud backup). You can remove saved lyrics at any time from the lyrics menu (“Reset imported lyrics”).

2. When the app uses the network

a) Update check. The app can check whether a newer version is available by downloading a small public file (update.json) hosted on GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information. No information about you or your music is sent.

b) Online lyrics search (optional). Only if you allow it and then press Search on the lyrics screen, the app sends the song title and artist name of the current song to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a community-run lyrics database, over HTTPS. LRCLIB's server also receives your IP address. Nothing else is sent — no file names or paths, no account, advertising or device identifiers. The app never searches automatically or in the background. You can withdraw your permission at any time in the lyrics menu (“Online lyrics search”). The lyrics that come back are written by LRCLIB's contributors and may belong to their rights holders; they are shown inside the app with their source, are not shared or exported by the app, and can be reported or removed (see section 5). LRCLIB handles data under its own terms; please see lrclib.net.

3. Permissions

  • Audio / storage read access — to find and play your music.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback) — to show playback controls and keep music playing with the screen off. Wake lock — to keep playback running while the screen is off.
  • Internet — only for the two cases in section 2.
  • Modify system settings — only if you choose “Use as sound” to set a song as a ringtone, notification or alarm sound.
  • Folder access you grant — if you pick a folder for .lrc lyrics files, the app reads lyrics files from that folder only. You can remove the folder at any time.

4. Data sharing, retention and children

We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not operate a server that receives your data. Data described in section 1 remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Your choices, reports and contact

You can use the app fully without the network features. To report lyrics that are wrong or that you believe infringe someone's rights, use “Report lyrics” in the lyrics menu (available for lyrics from an online search) or write to us; reported lyrics are removed from your device immediately. For any privacy question or request, contact: bd.protech.corp@gmail.com

6. Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example, new network features), this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
