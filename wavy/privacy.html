Wavy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · App: Wavy (com.bd.protech.wavy)

Wavy is an offline-first music player for Android. It plays the audio files that are already on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services. This page explains the small number of cases where the app uses the network, and what stays on your device.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your music library — the app reads your audio files through Android's media storage (permission to read audio). File names, tags and cover art are used only to show and play your music; they are never uploaded.
  • Listening history and statistics, favorites, playlists, queue and settings — stored locally in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Backups you create (optional) — Settings, Backup can save your listening statistics, favorites, playlists and settings into a file. You choose where the file is saved (for example Documents). The app does not upload it; keep it somewhere safe because it contains your listening history. Importing a backup replaces the data in the app with the contents of the file.
  • Custom cover art, imported lyrics and lyrics you chose from an online search — stored locally in the app's private storage (excluded from cloud backup). You can remove saved lyrics at any time from the lyrics menu (“Reset imported lyrics”).

2. When the app uses the network

a) Update check. The app can check whether a newer version is available by downloading a small public file (update.json) hosted on GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information. No information about you or your music is sent. The same file can also contain a switch that turns the online lyrics search (see b) off for all users.

b) Online lyrics search (optional). Only if you allow it and then press Search on the lyrics screen, the app sends the song title and artist name of the current song to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a community-run lyrics database, over HTTPS. LRCLIB's server also receives your IP address. Nothing else is sent — no file names or paths, no account, advertising or device identifiers. The app never searches automatically or in the background. You can withdraw your permission at any time in the lyrics menu (“Online lyrics search”). The lyrics that come back are written by LRCLIB's contributors and may belong to their rights holders; they are shown inside the app with their source, are kept only on your device, are not uploaded, shared or exported by the app, and can be reported or removed (see section 5). Wavy does not host or distribute lyrics and is not affiliated with LRCLIB. LRCLIB handles data under its own terms; please see lrclib.net.

c) Artist pictures (optional). Only if you allow it (the Artists tab asks once; you can change it any time in Settings, Library Management), the app sends the artist names from your library to Deezer (api.deezer.com) over HTTPS to find a picture for each artist, and saves the pictures on your device. Deezer's server also receives your IP address. Nothing else is sent. If you decline, nothing is sent and the Artists tab shows a generic icon. Pictures are provided by Deezer and belong to their rights holders; the app only displays them.

3. Permissions

  • Audio / storage read access — to find and play your music.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback) — to show playback controls and keep music playing with the screen off. Wake lock — to keep playback running while the screen is off.
  • Internet — only for the cases in section 2.
  • Modify system settings — only if you choose “Use as sound” to set a song as a ringtone, notification or alarm sound.
  • Folder access you grant — if you pick a folder for .lrc lyrics files, the app reads lyrics files from that folder only. You can remove the folder at any time.

4. Data sharing, retention and children

We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not operate a server that receives your data. Data described in section 1 remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Your choices, reports and contact

You can use the app fully without the network features.

Reporting lyrics. If lyrics are wrong or you believe they infringe someone's rights, use “Report lyrics” in the lyrics menu (available for lyrics from an online search). This removes the lyrics from your device immediately and opens an email draft addressed to us with the song title, artist and source. Nothing is sent automatically — the email leaves your device only if you send it. Reporting from the app does not by itself send a complaint to LRCLIB.

Rights holders. If you own rights in lyrics that can be found through the app, please write to the address below with the song, the artist and, if you have it, the LRCLIB record number shown in the report email. We will review it promptly and, where appropriate, stop offering the online lyrics search to all users.

For any privacy question or request, contact: bd.protech.corp@gmail.com

6. Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example, new network features), this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
